## Definition of done: what it must not do (fixed; answer each line with a test, or « n/a: why »)
<the builder writes, before the first commit, one line per item: the bad case for this lot and what must happen>
1. Coexistence: an interruption, network latency, a second click, a retry, two runs in parallel → no corrupted state,
   no panic, no double effect, no lost write.
2. Proof: each guard has a test that is red when the guard is cut; no test that cannot fail.
3. Claims: no text (comment, report, message, doc) says more than what runs; « will » for what doesn't run yet.
4. The three hostile worlds: an empty payload (null, []), a monstrous one, and a failure upstream → each proven by a
   test. The happy path counts for one third of the grade.
5. The other side: one source per fact; the caller and the callee agree (names, units, statuses).
6. Every state: loading, empty, error, a long text, a small screen → each says something true.
7. Rights and side effects: nobody sees or does what their role doesn't allow, one client's or instance's data stays
   its own, and nothing writes or sends what it wasn't asked to, including through what it loads or calls.
8. The real platform: what runs in the test runs the same on the real machine (its limits, its versions).
9. Silence: when it cannot do its job, it says so (an alarm, a non-zero exit, « not checked »), never a silent skip
   or a default that looks green.
10. Existing data and the deploy: the rows already in production, a migration on them, retention and deletion; merge
   = deploy (what goes first, and a sentence true only after another PR).
11. Zero waste: the run ends with a clean exit code, its temporary files removed and no orphan process or container
   left behind. If /tmp grew by 1 MB or a container lingers, the status is FAILED.
12. A surgical diff: no duplicated file (_v2, _new, _old, copy), no change outside the strict scope of the issue.
Plus the client's own lines: project.json `review_catches`.
A lot with no code (a demo, a count, a test on the platform, research) answers 3, 7, 8, 9 and 11 only.
