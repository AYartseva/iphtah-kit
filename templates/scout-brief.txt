was started in an area owned by someone else. « We are not paid to be smart, we are paid to create value. »

## Rules
- READ ONLY: no edit, no commit, no build, no test run, no install. No network except `gh` reads
  (`gh issue view`, `gh pr list`, `gh pr view`, `gh search`). Never post, push or message anyone.
- Cite file:line (or the route) for every entry of « exists ». Never answer « nothing exists » without having
  searched: put what you searched (the greps, the folders, the issue words) in « searched ».
- Built work may sit on local branches GitHub never saw: before « nothing exists », run
  `~/.claude/iphtah-kit/bin/shelf --client $client` and `git branch --list '*<row word>*'` in each repo for the row's
  words; a match there is « live »: branch.
- « live »: prod when the base branch carries it and it is deployed (say how you know; if unsure, merged);
  merged = on the base branch; branch = only on a branch or an open PR; no = written nowhere yet.
- Reuse before you write (rules file, rule 0b: $rules): list in « reuses » the tables, routes, components, rights
  and tools the work should build on.
- « touches »: every area of the table above that the work would change; « touchesOthers »: those whose owner is not
  $me.
- « verdict »: already-done when the row's goal is met (then say where); needs-discussion when it touches someone
  else's area or contradicts an issue; needs-spec when the row cannot be built as written; else ready.
- When « touchesOthers » is not empty, consider a SPLIT: our part, launchable now, and their part, to discuss with
  them. Propose one (verdict « split » and a « split » object) ONLY when the cut falls on a CONTRACT, a stable
  boundary such as an API field or a published file, and our half is useful and testable on its own with no change
  on their side. Otherwise do not split: use needs-discussion and say in « summary » why the row cannot be split.
  « split.theirs » names one of « touchesOthers » (same area and owner). The session checks a split before it is
  written; nothing is split automatically.
- SIZE SPLIT: when you size the work L or XL, you MUST also propose a « sizeSplit »: 2 to 4 pieces of
  size S or M, each written as what its user can do, the first one being the thinnest action a user can see (something a user
  can see exists within days, not at the end). Cut each piece on a stable boundary (a contract, a published
  file, a screen, a data kind), never in the middle of one; each piece useful and testable on its own (it can be merged and checked before the next starts);
  give their order with « after » (empty, or the name of an EARLIER piece). Big lots end beyond their forecast more
  often: each unknown dimension multiplies the space a lot must cover, and two small lots finish in time more often
  than one big. If the work truly cannot be cut, give no « sizeSplit » and say why in « summary ». No « sizeSplit »
  for XS, S or M. It may coexist with an area « split ».
- RULE QUESTIONS (pre-build review, 16/10/2026): list in « ruleQuestions » at most 3 questions whose answer would
  change what gets built, and only of two kinds: a real gap or ambiguity in the spec, or a yes needed on money,
  rights, words of outside people or an irreversible deletion. Each with its decider, what it changes and your
  recommendation. A question you would simply answer with your recommendation is not one: decide it, say « decided:
  X, because Y » in « summary », and put its useful follow-ups in « blockers » as issues to open (16/10/2026: « a
  question that doesn't really need me starts with "My recommendation" »). Omit the key when there is none.
- Stay under about 15 minutes. When time runs out, answer with what you have and say so in « blockers ».
- Scratch files, if any, only under $scratch/.
